Flow page — redesign sketches

The latest direction is Corporate Workbench: a reference-inspired green-and-charcoal palette, serif product heading and divider-led panels. It keeps the three-panel workspace and includes a sample replay, detail controls and Learn panel. This is a visual preview, not a service-connected application. Its Lucide icons require internet access.

Earlier explorations include Flat & Quiet, Compact and Airy — which push the minimalist direction further (flatter surfaces, quieter colour, fewer visible controls). Below them sit the original combined demo and the earlier explorations. These are self-contained static HTML; open any in a browser and resize to ~390px to see the mobile layout.

New · minimalist trio (built on the combined demo)

Direction 5 · minimal

Flat & quiet

The flagship restyle: drop shadows removed, boxes traded for hairline dividers, and the accent reserved for active / primary / busy only. Calm and near-monochrome.
  • Borderless surfaces, divider-led panels
  • Accent used sparingly; quieter greys
  • Same rail + chat + hero-diagram layout

Direction 6 · minimal

Compact

Dense and crisp — smaller radii, reduced padding, more information per screen. Advanced toggles collapse behind a “•••” overflow menu so the toolbar stays one row.
  • Tighter type scale & 5–6px radii
  • Single-row toolbar + overflow menu
  • Best for power users / small screens
Open desktop ↗
Direction 7 · minimal

Airy

The opposite end of minimal — generous whitespace, a larger type scale and the fewest visible controls. Advanced options live behind one calm “Options” menu; diagram-forward.
  • Editorial spacing & bigger type
  • Controls hidden behind an Options menu
  • Diagram as the unhurried hero

How to read these Sketches only — no Blazor, no build, no network. Once you pick a direction (or a mix), the next phase ports it into the real Flow page: evolve the tokens in Flow.razor.css, adjust the breakpoints, and restyle the FlowParts components. The fixed app/agent/user contributor colours are preserved in every direction because they encode meaning, not branding.
