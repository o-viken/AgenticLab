## Claude

**Claude** is Anthropic's family of large language models, and the name of the assistant
built on them. Claude is known for strong reasoning, **long context windows**, careful
instruction-following and reliable **tool use** — qualities that make it well suited to
agentic work.

- As an **assistant**, Claude chats, writes and analyses, and can call tools.
- As a **model**, it powers products like **Claude Code** and is available to developers
  through Anthropic's API and major cloud platforms.

Where the **LLM** concept explains language models in general, this is one specific family
of them. Pick the **Claude** logo to select its representative harness while keeping the shared workbench colors.
